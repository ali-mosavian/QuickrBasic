' The game's songs, packed into CHARLIE.DAT by tools/charlieart.py; the game plays any of them from its data alone.
' A song is "Song name:" and its DATA, in this order. "USE name" stands for the DATA of "Part name:".
' A zone's second act is its tune arranged again: a song of its own over the first's bars, a Part.
'   Its order, a letter a bar; each bar's voicing, a letter of its kit; the bars there are; the tempo; the bars the
'   bass waits; how loud the first chord voice, the lead, the second, the low double, the bass and the third voice
'   are, the third's 0 leaving it out; the bars at its end that fade; the bar it comes in at where it is not from
'   its start; 1 when it ends, 0 when it goes round; the octaves the lead is moved; 1 when it swings, a beat's
'   off-beat eighth on the triplet's last.
'   Its kit: the lead's sound, the bass's, the low double's and the third voice's. Then its voicings: how many, and
'   for each its letter, the first chord voice's sound, the second's, and 1 when the low double plays under them.
'   Then its drums: how many, and for each its letter, its sound, how loud, 7 when a fade rounds it up, its note.
'   Its bass riffs, its chord patterns and its drum patterns, each list after its count.
'   Its bars: the chord's root from D, a bass riff (from 1) and a drum pattern (from 0) by number, then the second
'   chord voice, the first, the lead and the third.
' A riff is 16 sixteenths: a note (0-9, a-z, semitones from D), a capital slid into, "-" holds it, "." rests; ""
' is a bar's rest. A second voice of two characters is a chord pattern by number and its note.
