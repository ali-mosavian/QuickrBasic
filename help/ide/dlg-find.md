# Find Dialog
keys: Find Dialog

Opened by [Find Command](Find Command).

    Find What                 the text to look for
    Match Upper/Lowercase     case must agree
    Whole Word                not part of a longer word

A whole word has no letter, digit or one of ! # $ % & . beside it.
OK finds the first match after the cursor and selects it.

See also [Repeat Last Find Command](Repeat Last Find Command),
[Change Dialog](Change Dialog).
